06 / QUALITY & TESTING

Product documentation.
Testing coordinated to your requirements.

Selected cookware samples from our supplier network have been tested by an independent laboratory against GB 4806.9-2023, China's food-contact standard for metal materials and articles. The reported sensory and metal migration test items met the specified requirements for those samples.

For U.S. buyers, we can coordinate additional product-specific testing and documentation with manufacturing partners and independent laboratories based on applicable FDA food-contact requirements, including relevant provisions of 21 CFR Parts 175–178 where applicable.
